Ratatoskr

A personal voice assistant that runs on your own Windows PC. In Norse myth, Ratatoskr is the squirrel who carries messages up and down Yggdrasil, the world tree. This Ratatoskr carries messages between you, your PC and your phone.

What it does

  • Answers questions by voice ("Hey Ratatoskr…") or by typing, using Anthropic's Claude.
  • Reminders, timers, lists, goals, people to keep in touch with, health and medication notes.
  • Optionally connects to Google Calendar, so you can ask what's on, add events, and move or cancel them.
  • Optionally reads and helps you reply to your email. Nothing is ever sent without your OK.
  • An optional phone app that talks to your own PC over your private network.

Google Calendar

If you choose to connect Google Calendar, Ratatoskr asks Google for permission to see and edit the events in your calendars. It uses that permission only to show your events, to add, move or cancel events when you ask, and to remind you about them. Your sign-in is kept on your own PC. You can disconnect at any time in Ratatoskr's Settings, or at myaccount.google.com/permissions.
