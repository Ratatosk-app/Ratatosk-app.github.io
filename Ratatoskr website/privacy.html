Privacy policy

Last updated: 17 November 2026

In short: Ratatoskr runs on your own PC and keeps your information there. It has no servers, accounts, ads or tracking of its own, and the person who made it receives none of your information. To answer you, it sends what you ask (and the details needed to answer it) to Anthropic's Claude service. If you connect Google Calendar, your events are used only to give you calendar features.

Who we are

Ratatoskr is a free, personal, non-commercial assistant program shared privately with friends. "We" means its maker. Contact: [YOUR SUPPORT EMAIL].

What stays on your PC

Your settings, chats, reminders, lists, goals, contacts you add, health notes, and the sign-ins for services you connect (such as Google) are saved in Ratatoskr's folder on your own computer. The Claude key is locked to your Windows account. Deleting the folder deletes this information.

What is sent elsewhere, and why

  • Anthropic (Claude). When you talk or type to Ratatoskr, your words and the information needed to answer them are sent to Anthropic's Claude API, which writes the reply. For example, if you ask "what's on tomorrow?", the relevant calendar events are included. If you switch on "Ratatoskr noticed", a short summary of your upcoming events, reminders and similar items is sent a few times a day so it can point out anything useful. Anthropic's own privacy policy applies to that service.
  • Google Calendar (only if you connect it). Ratatoskr talks directly to Google to read your events and to add, change or delete events when you ask it to.
  • Web search (only when a question needs it). Search words are sent through Anthropic's web search tool.
  • Email (only if you set it up). Ratatoskr connects directly to your email provider to read messages and, after you approve each one, send replies.
  • Your phone (only if you set it up). Your phone and PC talk over your own private Tailscale network. Notifications go through your phone browser's standard push service.
  • Free public information services such as weather and Wikipedia receive only what's needed, for example the name of your city for the weather.

Google user data

If you connect Google Calendar, Ratatoskr requests permission to view and edit events in your Google calendars. It uses this data only to:

  • show your events and answer your questions about them;
  • add, move or cancel events when you ask it to;
  • remind you about events and warn you about clashes.

Ratatoskr does not sell Google user data, does not use it for advertising, does not let anyone else read it, and does not use it to develop, improve or train generalized AI or machine-learning models. Calendar details are passed to Anthropic's Claude API only as part of answering you or giving you a feature you have switched on, as described above.

Ratatoskr's use and transfer of information received from Google APIs will adhere to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

Keeping and deleting your information

  • Everything Ratatoskr saves is in its folder on your PC. Delete the folder to delete it.
  • Disconnect Google at any time in Ratatoskr's Settings, or remove "Ratatoskr" at myaccount.google.com/permissions.
  • The maker of Ratatoskr never receives your data, so there is nothing to delete on our side.

Children

Ratatoskr is not intended for children under 13.
